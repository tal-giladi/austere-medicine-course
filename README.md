# Austere & War Medicine

Field medicine for EMT graduates who need to keep casualties alive when no help can reach them: during an attack, in a safe room for hours, or in a cut-off community for days. It teaches full tactical casualty care beyond EMT, prolonged field care, and low-resource medicine, each with improvised household-only methods.

> [!CAUTION]
> Performing these procedures on people without the required state certification is illegal. This course is for educational purposes only. Practise only on models and animal tissue.

## Who it is for

People who have finished an EMT course. EMT skills (tourniquet application, wound packing, chest seals, ABC assessment, IV insertion, START triage, field childbirth) are assumed and not re-taught.

## Structure

- Stage 1 — Under threat: war and attack trauma beyond EMT
- Stage 2 — Hours to days: prolonged field care
- Stage 3 — When the system is down: low-resource medicine

Every lesson ends in a drill or scenario, and every lesson shows the improvised version using household items only.
