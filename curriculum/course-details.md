# Course details

- Slug: `austere-medicine`
- Repo: `tal-giladi/austere-medicine-course` (public)
- Price: free. Visibility in the Academy: hidden (Tal, 2026-11-14).
- Risk notice: Performing these procedures on people without state certification is illegal; this course is for educational purposes only and practice is on models and animal tissue.
- One-line summary: Keep casualties alive when no help can reach you — war trauma beyond EMT, prolonged field care and low-resource medicine, with household improvisations.
- Who it is for: EMT graduates.
- Format: illustrated text lessons with several videos per procedure, home drills on models and kosher-butcher tissue, scenario practice, multiple-choice quizzes.
- Prerequisites: a completed EMT course.
- What you will learn:
  - Full TECC/TCCC care beyond EMT: tourniquet conversion and removal, hemostatics, junctional bleeding, pelvic binder.
  - Needle decompression, NPA/BVM and surgical cricothyroidotomy.
  - IO access, TXA, field pain relief and antibiotics with full doses.
  - Blast, head, eye, burn and abdominal injuries; CBRN decontamination.
  - Keeping a casualty alive for 72 hours: monitoring by hand, nursing, crush, sepsis.
  - Wound closure, abscess drainage and dislocation reduction.
  - Oral rehydration, outbreak control, chronic patients without medication, dental emergencies, psychological first aid.
  - Doing all of it with household items when nothing else is available.
- Home kit: see lesson 01.6 (kits) and each lesson's Practice section.
