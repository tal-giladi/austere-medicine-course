# Research notes (2026-11-14) — starting points, verify before use

Items marked UNVERIFIED were not confirmed by the researcher.

## TCCC / TECC
- TCCC Guidelines 01 May 2026 (Deployed Medicine; PDF https://learning-media.allogy.com/api/v1/pdf/18ccfdfc-a076-47e9-8a34-376efdd81b43/contents). 2026 changes: airway and TBI in TFC; analgesia (OTFC/fentanyl dropped from text; suzetrigine 100 mg PO then 50 mg q12h added; esketamine 14/28 mg IN added).
- Skill sets by tier (ASM/CLS/CMC/CPP), master list 25 Jan 2024, Army mods 3 Apr 2025: https://learning-media.allogy.com:443/api/v1/pdf/12faeecd-8aa4-46e6-8cbe-89a490ab3e32/contents
- C-TECC (civilian): Direct Threat / Indirect Threat / Evacuation Care; BLS/ALS split; pediatric v2.8 June 2024. 2025 update UNVERIFIED.
- Hemorrhage: TQ on skin 2-3 in above wound in TFC, second TQ side by side; Combat Gauze first choice (Celox, ChitoGauze, XStat, iTClamp alternatives), 3 min pressure; XStat never removed in field; junctional TQ (CM+); iTClamp head/neck, not within 1 cm of orbit.
- TQ conversion: all of: not in shock, can monitor wound, not an amputation. Try to convert within 2 h; don't remove after >6 h without monitoring/labs. 2026 note: ASM/CLS shouldn't convert after 2 h without advanced provider direction. Mark all times.
- Ukraine: Lukiianchuk et al. JTACS 2025, 74 pts TQ ≥4 h: 70.8% dialysis, 36.3% delayed amputation, 6.7% died. Patterson et al. "Life Over Limb" JSOM 2024 doi 10.55460/V057-2PCH.
- Pelvic binder indications: severe blunt/blast + pelvic pain, major lower-limb amputation, pelvic fracture signs, unconscious, or shock.
- Airway: sit up/lean forward; recovery position; NPA + BVM if SpO2 <90% (<92% TBI); surgical cric (bougie or open, flanged cuffed tube <10 mm OD, 6-7 mm ID), EtCO2 confirm, lidocaine if conscious. SGA removed 2024. No needle cric in TCCC.
- NDC: torso/blast trauma + progressive distress, tachypnea, unilateral breath sounds, SpO2 <90%, shock, or traumatic arrest. 14G or 10G, 3.25 in (8.25 cm). 5th ICS AAL or 2nd ICS MCL, never medial to nipple line. Over top of rib, to hub, hold 5-10 s, leave catheter. Success: improved distress, hiss, SpO2 ≥90%, return of consciousness/radial pulse. Fail → second at other site same side; recur → repeat same site; after 2 fails move to C. Traumatic arrest: bilateral NDC. Finger thoracostomy CP only.
- TXA 2 g slow IV/IO ASAP, ≤3 h. Calcium 1 g after first blood product (30 ml 10% Ca gluconate or 10 ml 10% CaCl2).
- Fluids preference: LTOWB > fresh whole blood (walking blood bank) > 1:1:1 > 1:1 > plasma or RBC. Endpoints radial pulse, mentation or SBP 100. Not in shock: oral fluids.
- Hypothermia: insulate from ground, remove wet, active heat on torso/axillae not skin, vapour barrier + hooded bag.
- TBI: SpO2 ≥92, SBP >100, EtCO2 35-45 / 10 breaths/min; head up 30° if not in shock; herniation → 250 ml 3%/5% HTS or 30 ml 23.4% over ≥10 min, repeat 20 min, max 2. Penetrating TBI: surface dressing only, no packing.
- Eye: visual acuity, rigid shield, ceftriaxone 2 g IV/IM or cefadroxil 1 g PO.
- Analgesia: acetaminophen 1000-1300 mg q8h + meloxicam 15 mg daily (+ suzetrigine); ketamine 100 mg IM / 50 mg IN / 25 mg (0.2-0.3 mg/kg) IV-IO over 1 min, repeat q30 min; ondansetron 4 mg q8h; no benzos with ketamine/opioids. Procedural sedation (CP): ketamine 1-2 mg/kg IV or 300 mg IM.
- Antibiotics (change 25-1): cefadroxil 1 g PO daily or cephalexin 500 mg q6h; ceftriaxone 2 g IV/IO/IM daily if no PO. Moxifloxacin/ertapenem phased out.
- Evisceration: rinse, one reduction attempt <60 s if no leak/bleeding, else impermeable cover, NPO.
- Burns: Rule of Nines to nearest 10%; >20% dry dressing + heat shell; Rule of Ten (%TBSA×10 ml/h for 40-80 kg, +100 ml/h per 10 kg above 80); oral fluids ≤30% if conscious; no antibiotics for burns alone. Traction splint removed 2024.
- CPR: not for blast/penetrating arrest in the field; bilateral NDC first.
- Card: DD1380; Israeli MIST handover.

## Prolonged care
- JTS PCC Guidelines (21 Dec 2021); PFC 10 core capabilities (SOMA PFC WG, JSOM 2015): monitor, resuscitate, ventilate/oxygenate, airway, sedation/pain, exam/diagnostics, nursing, surgical, telemedicine, prepare for evac. https://prolongedfieldcare.org/2015/02/09/10-essential-core-capabilities-for-prolonged-field-care/
- JTS CPG index: https://jts.health.mil/assets/docs/cpgs/CPG_Index.pdf (Nursing PCC 08 Jul 2025; Crush PFC 2016; Sepsis PFC 2020; Analgesia PFC 2017; Burns PFC 2017; Ocular PFC 2017; Wounds PFC 2017; Telemedicine 2023; Compartment Syndrome 21 May 2026; Hypothermia 2023; Hyperkalemia 2022).
- Nursing PCC (https://jts.health.mil/assets/docs/cpgs/Nursing_Interventions_PCC_08_July_2025_ID70_v1.1.pdf): vitals q4-12h by severity; I/O hourly if critical; urine in any bottle; UO ≥0.5 ml/kg/h; maintenance ~100-150 ml/h; food/shake q4-6h if alert; turn q2h (q1h hard surface), head up 30°; oral care q12h (q4h unconscious); bath daily; ROM; deep breathing hourly; flush locks q8h with ~5 ml; IO out by 24 h (max 48); dressings ≥ daily, irrigate with potable water, 10 cc syringe + 18G catheter; pulses q6h in splints; condom catheter / wicking alternatives.
- Crush (https://jts.health.mil/assets/docs/cpgs/Crush_Syndrome_PFC_28_Dec_2016_ID58.pdf): risk from ~1 h entrapment; IV before extrication, 2 L bolus then 1 L/h, target UO 100-200 ml/h; dark urine; hyperkalemia: Ca gluconate 10% 10 ml, insulin 10 U + D50 50 ml, albuterol neb; entrapment >2 h without crush protocol: 2 TQs before extrication.
- Sepsis without labs: fever/hypothermia, tachycardia, tachypnea, altered mentation, falling UO, mottling, spreading erythema; qSOFA (RR ≥22, altered mentation, SBP ≤100).
- Combat wounds stay open; delayed primary closure ~3-5 days if clean (standard teaching).
- Rectal fluids / hypodermoclysis volumes UNVERIFIED (hypodermoclysis ~1-1.5 L/24 h per site, palliative literature).

## Israel

## Low-resource
- ORS (WHO reduced osmolarity) mmol/L: Na 75, Cl 65, glucose 75, K 20, citrate 10 (245 mOsm/L); g/L NaCl 2.6, glucose 13.5, KCl 1.5, trisodium citrate dihydrate 2.9. Home: 1 L water, 6 level tsp sugar, ½ level tsp salt.
- WHO plans (MSF: https://medicalguidelines.msf.org/en/viewport/mme/english/appendix-14-plan-rehydration-who-32408338.html): A after each stool <2 y 50-100 ml, 2-10 y 100-200 ml; B 75 ml/kg over 4 h; C RL 100 ml/kg (<12 mo 30 ml/kg 1 h then 70 ml/kg 5 h; ≥12 mo 30 ml/kg 30 min then 70 ml/kg 2.5 h). Zinc 10 mg (2-6 mo) / 20 mg (6 mo-5 y) × 10-14 d. NG ORS 20 ml/kg/h × 6 h (verify).
- Malnutrition (WHO 2023): SAM MUAC <115 mm, MAM 115-<125 mm (6-59 mo).
- Burns austere (ABA 2016): oral resus <20% TBSA, many up to 40%; Parkland 4 ml/kg/%TBSA/24 h. WHO ORS + salt RCT 2016.
- MSF Clinical Guidelines ch. 10: dressings, wounds, burns, abscess I&D, etc. https://medicalguidelines.msf.org/viewport/CG/english/abscesses-18482406.html
- Sphere 2018: 15 L/person/day (7.5 survival), FRC 0.2-0.5 mg/L, ~1 toilet/20 people (verify).

## Grid-down extras
- Avulsed permanent tooth (IADT 2020): replant immediately; else milk > HBSS > saliva > saline; >60 min dry = PDL non-viable; never replant baby teeth.
- Dislocations (NOLS WFR protocols 2016, https://nols.edu/media/filer_public/3f/fc/3ffcc29b-afa6-461a-9f99-da063c9f3257/wild_med_practices_and_protocols_2016.pdf): anterior shoulder (external rotation, Cunningham, FARES, Stimson, scapular manipulation), lateral patella, digits; ankle realignment only with neurovascular compromise.
- Wound closure: don't close bites, punctures, crush/blast/GSW, contaminated, old (>6-12 h, face ~24 h), combat wounds.
- Chronic meds: T1DM without insulin → DKA ~1 day; steroid-dependent → adrenal crisis (hydrocortisone 100 mg IM); antiepileptic/benzo/alcohol withdrawal seizures; beta-blocker/clonidine rebound. Needs physician-level verification.
- SLEP (Lyon 2006, J Pharm Sci 95:1549): 88% of lots extended ≥1 y, mean 66 months, controlled storage only.
- PFA: WHO Look-Listen-Link; Farchi SIX Cs (https://asprtracie.hhs.gov/technical-resources/resource/7049/...); sixth C UNVERIFIED.
- Dead bodies: ICRC/PAHO/WHO Management of Dead Bodies after Disasters (2016).
- CBRN decon (US PRISM 2nd ed.): disrobe, dry decon (up to ~99%), then wet; nerve agent signs SLUDGE/DUMBELS, miosis; atropine+oxime autoinjector.
- Curricula: NOLS WFR; Ranger Medic Handbook 2025.
