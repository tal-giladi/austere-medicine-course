# Publishing warning

Review before making this course public:

- It teaches invasive procedures (needle decompression, surgical cricothyroidotomy, intraosseous access, suturing, abscess drainage, dislocation reduction) and full prescription-drug doses, which are outside lay and EMT scope in Israel. Every lesson carries a legal/educational-use warning.
- Module 6 covers attack scenes and CBRN (nerve agents, decontamination). It contains no weapon or attack content, only casualty care.
- Medical facts were checked against current guidelines (TCCC 2026, JTS, WHO/MSF) on the dates in each lesson; a clinician review is recommended before a public release.
