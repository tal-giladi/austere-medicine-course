# Module 9 quiz: Wound closure and minor surgery

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on everything in Module 9: deciding whether to close a wound or leave it open, tape, glue and hair apposition, local anaesthetic doses and the digital block, suturing, staples and removal times, abscess drainage, foreign bodies, and doing all of it with household needles, thread, blades and gloves. Each question is a field decision, not a definition. The pass mark is 70%.

Before you start, complete the module scenario in lesson 09.6 (the cut-off moshav on day 3, practised on chicken or beef tongue). Keep a calculator at hand for doses.
