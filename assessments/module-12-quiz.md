# Module 12 quiz: Medicine without a doctor

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on everything in Module 12: sorting feverish adults, children and babies by danger, meningococcal disease and sepsis in infants, choosing and dosing antibiotics for chest, urine, skin, ear, throat and gut infections, keeping people with diabetes, adrenal insufficiency, epilepsy and asthma alive without their usual supply, storing and substituting medication including insulin without a fridge, dental emergencies, and eye, ear and nose problems up to a nosebleed that will not stop. Each question is a field decision, not a definition. The pass mark is 70%.

Before you start, complete the module practical in lesson 12.6: your household medication reserve plan and the 10-day cut-off scenario.
