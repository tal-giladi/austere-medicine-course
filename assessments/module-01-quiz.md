# Module 1 quiz

This quiz checks whether you can make the first decisions of austere and threat care in realistic Israeli situations: judging how long you will be alone, which phase of care you are in, what to do first, how the law applies to you, what your kits must hold, and how to document and hand over. It has 10 scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Each answer comes with a short explanation after you submit.

The practical piece for this module is the scenario in lesson 01.7: finish your kits and document three casualties from a rocket strike through to handover. Do it before taking this quiz.
