# Module 4 quiz: Circulation, resuscitation and field drugs

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on everything in Module 4: recognising and resuscitating shock, intraosseous access, TXA and calcium, whole blood, pain relief, antibiotics, routes and dose calculations, and the risks of sedation and drug combinations. Each question is a field decision, not a definition. The pass mark is 70%.

Before you start, complete the module scenario in lesson 04.8 (your drug card, the 20 dosing drills and the injection practice). Keep a calculator and paper at hand.
