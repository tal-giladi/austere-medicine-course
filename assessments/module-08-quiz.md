# Module 8 quiz: What goes wrong after the first hour

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on everything in Module 8: crush syndrome and fluids before extrication, tourniquets before release, dangerous potassium without a monitor, compartment syndrome without a surgeon, rhabdomyolysis, wound infection and sepsis without labs, and wound care from day 1 onward, including Dakin's solution, sugar and honey dressings and delayed closure. Each question is a field decision, not a definition. The pass mark is 70%.

Before you start, complete the module scenario in lesson 08.6 (the collapsed-building casualty from extrication to day 3). Keep a calculator at hand for doses and dilutions.
