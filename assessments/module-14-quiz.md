# Module 14 quiz: Capstone, 72-hour scenarios

This is the final quiz of the course. It has 10 scenario questions that span all fourteen modules: each one drops you into a moment from a safe room, a collapsed building or a cut-off shelter and asks for the decision that keeps the casualty alive, using the rules from the lesson that teaches it. The pass mark is 70%.

Before you start, play all three capstone scenarios to their scoring rubric, ideally once with a kit and once household-only.

If you miss a question, the explanation names the lesson it comes from. Revisit the scenario that uses it:

- Triage, tourniquet reassessment and conversion, burns in a child, delayed blast injury, safe-room safety, handover: [14.1 · Safe room after an attack: 8 hours, 3 casualties, no evacuation](../lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md)
- Long tourniquets, the crush protocol, potassium, kidney failure and overload, compartment syndrome, wound sepsis, running a casualty point for days: [14.2 · Collapsed building: crush, long tourniquet, day-2 infection](../lessons/module-14/lesson-02.md)
- Diarrhoea plans and cholera control, chlorine and water, insulin substitution and sick-day rules, DKA, dental abscess and Ludwig's angina, choosing who is evacuated: [14.3 · Cut-off shelter: diarrhoea outbreak, a diabetic without insulin, a dental abscess](../lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md)
