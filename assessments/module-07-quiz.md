# Module 7 quiz: Keeping a casualty alive for 72 hours

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on everything in Module 7: organising prolonged care, monitoring and reading trends, fluids in and out, nursing care, lines and dressings, pain and sedation over days, telemedicine, and preparing for evacuation. Each question is a decision you would make during a long hold, not a definition. The pass mark is 70%.

Before you start, run the 24-hour moshav scenario in lesson 07.8 at least once on a paper flowsheet.
