# Module 11 quiz: Fluids, diarrhoea and outbreaks

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on everything in Module 11: why salt and glucose together rehydrate, making ORS from sachets, salt and sugar or rice and checking it, WHO Plans A, B and C by weight with zinc for children, nasogastric and subcutaneous fluids when a person cannot drink, cholera treatment and antibiotics, running an outbreak in a shelter with chlorine solutions from household bleach, Sphere water and toilet minimums with household water treatment, and MUAC screening for malnutrition. Each question is a field decision, not a definition. The pass mark is 70%.

Before you start, complete the module scenario in lesson 11.7 (the community centre after the earthquake).
