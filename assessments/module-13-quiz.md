# Module 13 quiz: People, the dead, and running a casualty point for days

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on everything in Module 13: psychological first aid with the SIX Cs and WHO Look-Listen-Link, panic and children at a scene, your own stress, sleep and moral injury, running a casualty collection point for days with SALT triage, re-triage, expectant care, rationing, helpers, records and shifts, and handling the dead with respect, identification and storage. Each question is a field decision, not a definition. The pass mark is 70%.

Before you start, complete the module practical in lesson 13.4: the body-handling drill, your shift plan for a 3-day casualty point, and the scenario.
