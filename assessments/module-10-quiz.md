# Module 10 quiz: Dislocations and orthopaedics without X-ray

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on everything in Module 10: recognising and reducing an anterior shoulder dislocation with low-force techniques and self-reduction, kneecap, finger and toe dislocations, the true knee dislocation as a vascular emergency, realigning an ankle fracture-dislocation when the foot or skin is at risk, why elbow and hip dislocations are splinted rather than reduced in the field, long-term splint care, the Ottawa rules, and the decision to walk out, carry or stay. Each question is a field decision, not a definition. The pass mark is 70%.

Before you start, complete the module scenario in lesson 10.4 (the hill path after the earthquake).

- Kneecap reduction, knee dislocation and the popliteal artery, finger, knuckle, thumb and toe dislocations, buddy taping: [10.2 · Kneecap, finger and toe dislocations](../lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md)
- Skin tenting and the threatened foot, ankle realignment, haematoma block, open fracture-dislocations, elbow and hip recognition: [10.3 · Ankle and fracture-dislocations: realignment when circulation is at risk](../lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)
- Immobilisation times, daily splint care, clots and nerve pressure, the Ottawa ankle, foot and knee rules, walking out, crutches: [10.4 · Long-term splint care and walking out](../lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md)
