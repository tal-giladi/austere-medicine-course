# Module 6 quiz: Attack scenes, CBRN and moving casualties

This quiz has 10 scenario questions on everything in Module 6: reading an attack scene and choosing where to treat, moving casualties with drags, carries and improvised litters, recognising and treating nerve agent poisoning, decontamination for one person and for many, and smoke, carbon monoxide and cyanide. Each question is a field decision, not a definition. The pass mark is 70%.

Before you start, complete the module scenario in lesson 06.5 (the extraction drill and the CBRN decision scenario). Keep a calculator at hand for doses.

If you miss a question, revisit the lesson it comes from:

- Zones, second devices and strikes, fire, collapse, choosing a casualty collection point, treating during alerts: [06.1 · Scene threats and where to treat](../lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md)
- Drags, carries, improvised litters, stairs, safe rooms and private cars: [06.2 · Moving casualties: drags, carries, litters and vehicles](../lessons/module-06/lesson-02.md)
- CRESS, mild and severe signs, autoinjectors, atropine endpoint, child doses, Israeli civilian kits: [06.3 · Nerve agents: recognition and autoinjectors](../lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)
- Disrobe, dry and wet decontamination, wounds, eyes, hair, hypochlorite, groups and children: [06.4 · Decontamination: disrobe, dry, wet](../lessons/module-06/lesson-04.md)
- Carbon monoxide, cyanide, hydroxocobalamin, oxygen and escaping smoke: [06.5 · Smoke, carbon monoxide and cyanide](../lessons/module-06/lesson-05.md)
